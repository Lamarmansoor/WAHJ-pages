01

Introduction and acceptance

The site provides information about WAHJ and its technology. These terms describe rules for using the website and its content; a visit alone is not treated as express contractual acceptance of a paid service. If software services or subscriptions become available, the applicable contract terms and how to accept them will be presented separately.

02

Service and website scope

WAHJ presents technology for managing beauty businesses. This site describes systems, plans and illustrative workflows. A sample dashboard is not necessarily connected to a real salon’s data and does not mean every feature is immediately available.

Operational software services and salon clients’ data are distinct from marketing content. The parties’ rights and obligations for those services will be set out in an offer or separate agreement when available.

03

Using the website

You may browse the content and follow its links for lawful purposes. Do not disrupt the site, bypass its protections, access data or systems without authorization, submit automated or misleading requests, or provide content that infringes others’ rights. Harmful use may be restricted under applicable law without limiting statutory rights.

04

Intellectual property

The WAHJ brand, logos, site design, text and graphics present the service. Rights remain with their lawful owners. Visiting does not grant a licence to copy, republish or commercially exploit content beyond what law or the rights holder permits.

Third-party rights in elements displayed on the site are respected. These terms do not transfer ownership of software or data governed by separate service agreements.

05

Accuracy of information and features

Marketing information should reflect the approved service scope. Sample data and dashboards may illustrate workflows but are not real records or measurements of a particular business.

Final plan details, available functions and service conditions are determined by the approved offer or agreement at contracting. This explanation does not remove a duty to provide accurate information or any statutory right.

06

Free trial

The site advertises an approved 14-day trial period to explore WAHJ. Self-service account creation and trial activation are not technically available in the reviewed state. Clicking a button or requesting a demo does not automatically start a trial or subscription.

Start conditions, scope and activation method will be disclosed before the trial is actually offered. No further obligations arise solely from displaying its duration.

07

Demo requests and contact

A demo request or initial enquiry is not a purchase or acceptance of a paid subscription. In the environment covered by the operational report, delivery of the demo form to an active recipient was not established. Confirm the availability of a contact channel before relying on a form submission.

You may open WhatsApp and review a prepared message there; it is sent only if you choose to send it within that service. If official form intake is activated, these terms and the privacy policy will be updated to reflect the actual channel.

08

Plans and prices

Prices are displayed in Saudi riyals (SAR). Before a paid service becomes available for purchase, review the plan description and features, total price, applicable taxes or charges and contracting steps.

Final contractual details are stated in the applicable offer or agreement. This draft creates no unapproved charge or obligation, subject to disclosures required by the Saudi E-Commerce Law where applicable.

09

Subscriptions and payments

The site does not currently offer an active paid checkout or electronic collection flow. Browsing plans or requesting a demo creates no payment obligation and does not constitute consent to renewal or automatic charges.

Subscription duration, payment and delivery steps, and any renewal mechanism need commercial and legal approval and clear disclosure before activation. This draft creates no binding terms for them.

10

Cancellation and refunds

No cancellation or refund policy for a paid website subscription has been approved. It must be settled, legally reviewed and disclosed before paid subscriptions are enabled, while respecting statutory consumer rights. The absence of an approved policy does not remove a statutory right.

11

Links and external services

Some links may take you outside the site, for example to WhatsApp. Review that service’s terms and privacy policy before sending information through it; the transition is your choice. This section does not exclude liability imposed on a provider by law.

12

Limits of liability

Marketing content explains the service, and inaccuracies should be corrected under applicable requirements. Illustrative examples and dashboards are not a promise of a particular operational outcome or a contractual obligation beyond an approved agreement.

Any limitation is interpreted under applicable Saudi law and does not exclude a liability or right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

13

Applicable laws

Saudi Arabia is the site’s current target market. The provider identity and any contractual jurisdiction wording will be reviewed by Saudi legal counsel after the entity and commercial terms are approved.

These terms do not limit consumer or data-subject rights under applicable law, including the E-Commerce Law and Personal Data Protection Law where relevant.

14

Changes and contact

The terms may be updated if the site’s scope changes or a new service becomes available. The approved version and its actual last-update date will be shown once adopted. The official contact channel and provider details will be announced when approved. This document remains a draft while required disclosures and paid-service terms are pending.
