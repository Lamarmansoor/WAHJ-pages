All FAQs
01Is WAHJ only for bookings?+

WAHJ is more than a booking system. It is a connected platform for managing your salon, from appointments, client records, and services to point of sale, team follow-up, and reporting. These tools help bring daily work into a more organized flow, according to the plan you choose.

02What can I manage in one place?+

Imagine having one place to follow client bookings, organize salon services, manage sales and staff, and review how your business is doing. WAHJ brings these workflows together, with inventory, accounting, and HR tools available according to the plan, so you spend less time switching between systems and more time growing your business.

03Is WAHJ right for my type of business?+

WAHJ is designed for beauty and care businesses, including hair and nail salons, beauty centers, spas, and beauty studios. You can organize services and appointments around the way your business works, then choose the tools and plan that best fit your needs.

04Does WAHJ support Arabic and English?+

The WAHJ website is available in Arabic and English, so you can explore the platform and its features in your preferred language. The languages supported inside the product itself should be confirmed during a product walkthrough before subscribing.

05How do I choose the right plan?+

Choose based on your salon’s needs and stage of growth. Starter is a starting point for salon management, bookings, and point of sale. Growth adds inventory, accounting, and a website for accepting bookings. Pro covers a broader scope that includes HR, the manager app, and operationally ready WAHJ AI insights; confirm readiness of the other systems before subscribing. Explore plans and pricing

06Can I try WAHJ before subscribing?+

The approved trial period is 14 days, but self-service sign-up and trial activation are not technically available yet. Contact the WAHJ team to learn about the available options and explore tools for your business; requesting a demo does not automatically start a trial. Learn about the free trial
