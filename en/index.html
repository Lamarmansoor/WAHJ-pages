All FAQs
01Is WAHJ only for bookings?+

No. It brings bookings, client records, team management, point of sale, and reporting together so you can follow daily operations in one place.

02What can I manage in one place?+

You can follow appointments, services, clients, your team, sales, and reports through WAHJ instead of spreading daily work across separate tools.

03Is WAHJ suited to my type of business?+

WAHJ is designed for salons, spas, care centers, and beauty studios. The tools you use can vary with the way your business operates.

04Can I use WAHJ in Arabic and English?+

Yes. The WAHJ website is available in both Arabic and English.

05How do I choose the right plan?+

Compare the systems included in each plan, then book a demo to review your needs and confirm which features are ready before subscribing.

06Can I explore WAHJ before choosing?+

Yes. Book a demo to discuss your day-to-day operations and review plan scope and available features.
