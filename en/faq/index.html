FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you begin.

What you need to know to decide whether WAHJ fits the way your business works.

Is WAHJ only a booking system?+

No. WAHJ brings bookings, clients, team, payments, invoices, and business visibility into one platform.

Can it work for a single-location business?+

Yes. Start with your current business size and explore options as your team and locations grow.

Will my team need extensive training?+

WAHJ is organized around clear everyday tasks to help teams get familiar with the workflow.

Do I need special hardware?+

WAHJ is cloud-based and can be accessed from your existing internet-connected devices.

Does WAHJ support WhatsApp?+

A WhatsApp contact option appears when an official support number is enabled in the site configuration.

Can I see a demonstration before choosing a plan?+

Yes. Request a demo to discuss your business and your team’s needs.
