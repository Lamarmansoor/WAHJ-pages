01

Introduction and scope

This policy explains how Smart Middar Solutions W.L.L. (Commercial Registration No. 185325), as the provider of WAHJ, handles personal data when you visit the WAHJ website, use its services, or contact its team. It applies alongside the Terms of Use and any service-specific notice or agreement.

WAHJ includes a website and cloud services for beauty businesses. The provider’s role may vary depending on the data and the purpose for which it is processed.

02

Responsible entity and processing roles

Smart Middar Solutions W.L.L. (Commercial Registration No. 185325), provider of WAHJ, is responsible for data it collects for its own operations, such as account administration, billing, support, security and communications. For privacy enquiries, contact support@middar.com.

When a salon uses WAHJ to manage data about its clients, staff, branches or transactions, WAHJ processes that data on the salon’s behalf to provide the service. The salon is responsible for the lawful collection and entry of that data and for providing notices and obtaining required consents. The provider supports the salon within its role and obligations.

03

Data we may collect

Depending on how you use the service, data may include your name, username, account and business details; email, phone number, address and communication preferences; and information about branches, staff, clients, suppliers, services, products, appointments, orders, invoices, inventory, point-of-sale activity and reports. Billing and payment details needed for subscriptions or settlement may also be processed.

Technical data may include IP address, device and browser type, language, session and error logs, and performance information, as well as support messages and files you send during setup, training or support. Do not send information the service does not need or full payment-card details through support channels.

04

Data sources and purposes

Data is collected when you register, request a demo or contact the team; when a customer or its users enter information into WAHJ; and through use of the website, platform and integrations you choose to enable.

Data is used to provide and operate the service and accounts, configure it and train and support users, administer subscriptions and billing, protect the platform and prevent fraud, handle requests and complaints, meet legal obligations, maintain and improve the service, and analyze performance where permitted by law. We may send service updates or marketing where lawful; you may object to direct marketing.

05

Legal bases

Data is processed as necessary to fulfil your request or contract and provide the service, comply with legal obligations, with your consent where required, or for legitimate interests such as service security and improvement, subject to data-subject rights and applicable law.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it. Withdrawal does not affect earlier processing or processing that relies on another lawful basis.

06

Sharing and transfers

Data may be shared as needed with Middar-related entities; hosting, cloud infrastructure, support, security and technical providers; payment providers or integrations you choose to enable; professional advisers; and authorities where required or permitted by law. Personal data is not sold as a standalone product.

Data may be processed in Bahrain or elsewhere depending on the service and its providers. Where data is transferred across borders, safeguards required by applicable law are used. Independent third-party services are subject to their own policies and terms.

07

Cookies and similar technologies

The website or platform may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies to remember preferences and language, manage sessions and sign-in, improve security, measure performance and enhance the user experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling some may affect service functionality.

08

Retention and protection

We keep data for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by legal, accounting, contractual or security obligations. When the purpose ends, data is deleted, destroyed, isolated or de-identified unless there is a lawful reason to retain it longer.

We use reasonable technical, organizational and administrative measures to protect data, including access controls, separation of permissions, monitoring, backups and incident response. No electronic method guarantees absolute security.

09

Data-subject rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to learn the purposes of processing, access your data or obtain a copy, request correction, updating or deletion where applicable, withdraw consent, or object to direct marketing. These rights may be subject to legal limitations.

Send privacy requests or complaints to support@middar.com. If data concerns a salon’s clients or staff and is processed on the salon’s behalf, please contact the salon first; the service provider may assist it within its role.

10

Children, updates and contact

WAHJ is intended for businesses and is not designed for children. It is not intended to collect children’s data directly, except as part of a lawful use managed by a customer that bears responsibility for it.

We may update this policy when services or legal requirements change. The updated version and its effective date will appear on this page. Contact support@middar.com or visit https://www.middar.com.
